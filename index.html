🌙 Lunar Outpost: Survive the Night

Set near the Moon’s south pole, the region NASA studies for Artemis III landing sites. Keep 4 crew alive for 30 days, one full Moon cycle. Assign your crew-hours each day, and keep the base between 10 and 30 °C. Everything you do costs power, and the lunar night lasts about 14.75 Earth days (half of NASA’s 29.5-day lunar cycle).
